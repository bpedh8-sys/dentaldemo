About Lumire

A studio built around
your comfort

Founded in 2009 by Dr. Elena Voss, Lumire began with a simple belief — dental care should feel less like a procedure and more like a ritual of self-care. Our studio blends the calm of a spa, the precision of a laboratory, and the warmth of a familiar face.

Every detail, from the scent in the air to the technology in our hands, is chosen with intention. We don't just treat teeth — we craft experiences that change how you feel about the dentist.

Gentle Approach

Painless techniques for anxious patients.

Advanced Tech

3D imaging, digital scans, laser dentistry.

Personalized Plans

Treatment designed around your life.

Spa-Like Calm

Aromatherapy, blankets, noise-canceling.
